From idea to live, in five steps

Every post moves through the same steps. Mark each step in the post's brief, and the calendar shows what's stuck.

  1. 1PlanBrief is agreed. You get the 5 am reminder the day before.
  2. 2CreateFilm or design it during the 5–9 am prep window.
  3. 3ReadyMedia is final, caption and hashtags copied.
  4. 4ScheduledBooked in Meta Business Suite, LinkedIn and Pinterest at the times shown. They publish on their own.
  5. 5PostedCheck it went live, reply to comments in the first hour.

SEO in every post

  • Service and city keyword in the first line
  • Link to the service page, not the homepage
  • Keyword file names, like heavy-haulage-sydney.jpg
  • Location tag on every post

Reuse everything

  • Reels go to Instagram, Facebook and YouTube Shorts
  • Carousels become LinkedIn PDFs and Pinterest pins
  • The best post each month becomes a blog article
  • Rewrite the first line for each platform

Grows

    Doesn't grow

      Check the "cheapest" claim. The website calls Alpha Trucking the "cheapest freight company". Under Australian Consumer Law that claim needs to be backed up. In posts, use "competitive rates" or "fair, upfront pricing".
      Measure

      Know what's working.

      Reach

      Reel views, accounts reached and new followers each week. Look for steady growth on Instagram.

      Saves & shares

      These count more than likes. A post with lots of saves is a format to repeat.

      Leads

      Website clicks, WhatsApp messages, calls to 1300 025 742 and quote forms. Ask every new customer where they found you.

      Local SEO

      Google Business calls and direction requests, and Search Console clicks on route pages.
