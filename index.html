The plan in one screen

What you post, and why

Alpha Trucking sells to two very different audiences. Businesses that ship freight (warehouse managers, e-commerce owners, builders, importers) decide on LinkedIn and Google. The wider public, future drivers and local businesses discover you on Instagram and Facebook through short video. The plan runs both tracks side by side.

40%

Show the work

Real trucks, real loads, real depot. Time-lapses of loading, heavy haulage moves, 4am starts at North St Marys. This is your main growth engine.

25%

Teach shippers

Pallet packing, container sizes, parcel vs pallet vs FTL, dangerous goods basics, insurance. Saveable carousels that rank and get shared.

20%

Sell the routes

Sydney–Melbourne, Sydney–Adelaide, Melbourne–Brisbane, Gold Coast–Brisbane, plus 3PL, containers and DG. Each post links to a service page for SEO.

15%

People & trust

Drivers, reviews, safety, wellbeing, milestones. This builds the credibility behind a "fastest growing" claim.

Weekly rhythm

  • 2 Reels (Instagram + Facebook)
  • 1 carousel (Instagram, re-cut as a LinkedIn PDF)
  • 2 LinkedIn posts (Tue and Thu mornings)
  • 1 Google Business Profile post
  • 3–5 Pinterest pins from the week's graphics
  • Stories on most weekdays (polls, depot snaps)

What to film every week

  • One loading or unloading time-lapse
  • One truck leaving or arriving on a route
  • One 20-second driver or staff clip
  • 5–10 clean photos of trucks and freight

Batch this on one morning. It feeds the whole week.

SEO rules for every post

  • Put the service + city keyword in the first line
  • Link to the matching service page, not the homepage
  • Name photo files with keywords (e.g. heavy-haulage-sydney.jpg)
  • Add a location tag (North St Marys / the route city)
  • Turn the best posts into a blog post each month

Content that grows, and content that doesn't

Grows or not

Ratings are based on how these formats usually perform for transport and trade businesses. Your own insights after the first month decide what stays.

Grows the page

  • Loading / unloading time-lapse ReelsHighSatisfying to watch, gets rewatched, which pushes reach.
  • Heavy haulage and oversize movesHighBig machinery on a truck is the most shared trucking content.
  • Driver POV and "day in the life"HighFaces build followers and attract drivers you may hire.
  • How-to carousels (packing, sizes, which service)HighGet saved and shared, and rank in Instagram and Pinterest search.
  • Christmas cut-off and "book early" postsHighDirect buying intent. Brings quote requests.
  • Customer case studies with real resultsHighBest LinkedIn format for leads.

Doesn't grow (use sparingly)

  • "Happy Monday" quotes and stock photosLowNo reason to stop scrolling. Skip entirely.
  • Plain "we offer freight services" ads as postsLowReads as an ad, gets low reach. Put the offer inside a tip or a story.
  • Holiday greeting graphics with just the logoLowFine once, but pair them with a real photo of your team.
  • Reposting the same image on every platform unchangedLowResize and rewrite the first line for each platform.
  • Opening hours and closure noticesLowWon't grow the page, but customers need them. Keep them on GBP and Stories.
  • Hashtag walls of 25+ tagsLowUse 5–8 specific tags. Keywords in the caption now matter more.
Check the "cheapest" claim. The website calls Alpha Trucking the "cheapest freight company". Under Australian Consumer Law, a claim like that needs to be backed up. On social posts, safer wording is "competitive rates", "fair, upfront pricing" or "get a quote in minutes".
Measure monthly

How to tell if it's working

Reach

Reel views, accounts reached, new followers per week. Aim for steady week-on-week growth on Instagram.

Engagement

Saves and shares count more than likes. A post with many saves is a format to repeat.

Leads

Website clicks, WhatsApp messages, calls to 1300 025 742, quote form fills. Ask every new customer "where did you hear about us?"

SEO

Google Business Profile calls and direction requests, and Search Console clicks on route and service pages.

At the end of each month, find your top 3 posts by saves + shares and make three more like them. Drop the bottom 3 formats.
